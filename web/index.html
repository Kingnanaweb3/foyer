Hi! I'm the front desk for Riverside Community Trust. Ask me about events, parking, sign-ups, or your donations.

Two jobs nobody has time for.

A small nonprofit runs on volunteers. Nobody is paid to answer the same question for the fortieth time, and nobody is watching the donor list closely enough to notice when someone stops giving.

The questions never stop

When is it. Where do I park. Can I still sign up. Is there a waitlist. Every one is easy, every one needs answering, and they arrive while the people who could answer them are running the actual event.

In the demo data: 2 events, 1 already at capacity, 50 spots to track by hand.

Donors leave quietly

Nobody sends a note to say they have stopped giving. They just stop, and six months later someone notices. The window to say something useful has already closed.

In the demo data: James Okafor last gave 194 days ago, against a 45-day pattern. Nobody flagged it.
