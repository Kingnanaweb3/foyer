Riverside Community Trust Usually replies instantly

Hi! I'm the front desk for Riverside Community Trust. Ask me about events, parking, sign-ups, or your donations.

Two jobs nobody has time for.

The questions never stop

When is it. Where do I park. Can I still sign up. Is there a waitlist. Every one is easy, every one needs answering, and they arrive while the people who could answer them are running the actual event.

In the demo data: 2 events, 1 already at capacity, 50 spots to track by hand.

Donors leave quietly

Nobody sends a note to say they have stopped giving. They just stop, and six months later someone notices. The window to say something useful has already closed.

In the demo data: James Okafor last gave 194 days ago, against a 45-day pattern. Nobody flagged it.

One message in. Four decisions out.

01

The orchestrator reads the message and picks a specialist

It does not answer anything itself. A question about parking goes to the Front Desk. A question about a donor goes to the Donor Steward. Both sub-agents are exposed to it as tools, so routing is a decision it makes and logs, not a keyword match.

ask_front_deskask_donor_steward
02

The Front Desk acts on its own

Event times, parking, remaining spots, sign-ups. It can write an RSVP straight to the roster and refuse one when an event is full. These are small and reversible, so nobody needs to approve them.

get_event_infomake_rsvp
03

The Donor Steward drafts, but cannot send

It reads giving history, spots donors who have drifted past their own pattern, and writes the message. Then it stops. There is no send tool in its hands to reach for — the draft goes to the approval queue with the reasoning that produced it, and waits.

get_donor_profilecheck_lapsing_donorsdraft_followup_messagequeue_approval
04

Every action lands in one log

What the agent did, why it did it, and what the human decided — written in language a board member can read without a technical briefing. Approvals and rejections go in the same trail as the agent's own actions.

log_decision
